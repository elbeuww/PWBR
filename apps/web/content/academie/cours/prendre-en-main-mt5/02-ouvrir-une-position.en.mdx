Once connected on demo, the second lesson opens a practice position. The goal is the action, not the outcome: we drill the mechanics of the order.

## Place the order

<Steps>
1. Double-click the instrument in the "Market Watch" window.
2. Choose "Market execution" for this first attempt.
3. Set the volume to the smallest value offered.
4. Click "Sell by Market" or "Buy by Market".
</Steps>

<Callout variant="astuce">
Always start with the minimum volume on demo. The point is to watch how the position appears in the "Trade" tab, not to chase a gain.
</Callout>

## Read the open position

The "Trade" tab lists the position with its entry price and floating result. That result changes on every tick: this is normal, and it is exactly what a stop-loss will frame in the next lesson.
